# ATLAS Stage 134 — Live Physical-World Intelligence and Real-Time Geospatial Observation System

## 1. Executive Summary & Architecture Overview

ATLAS Stage 134 establishes the institutional-grade **Live Physical-World Intelligence Engine**, bringing continuous, source-grounded observation of dynamic physical entities into the ATLAS quantitative research operating system.

### Core Observation Layers
1. **Live Aircraft Tracking (ADS-B)**: Ingests transponder state vectors (OpenSky Network / FAA / ADS-B Exchange), including ICAO 24-bit addresses, callsigns, barometric altitude, true track, ground velocity, squawks, and vertical rates.
2. **Live Maritime Vessels (AIS)**: Ingests maritime position and voyage reports (Spire Maritime / USCG / MarineTraffic), tracking MMSI, IMO, vessel dimensions, draught, speed over ground (SOG), course over ground (COG), destination, and ETA.
3. **Active Satellites & Space Stations (SGP4 Propagation)**: Ingests Keplerian orbital elements (CelesTrak / Space-Track) and computes instantaneous geodetic subpoints, altitude, velocity, and ground footprint coverage with secular $J_2$ perturbations.
4. **Severe Weather Alerts (CAP v1.2)**: Ingests NOAA / NWS Common Alerting Protocol emergency polygons, severity classifications (`EXTREME`, `SEVERE`, `MODERATE`, `MINOR`), certainty, and active validity windows.
5. **Surface Weather Observations (METAR)**: Ingests global airport weather station observations (temperature, dew point, pressure, wind velocity/gusts, visibility).
6. **Real-Time Global Earthquakes (USGS)**: Ingests global seismic hypocenters with idempotent revision upserts (e.g. magnitude upgrades) and tsunami flags.
7. **Active Wildfires & Thermal Hotspots (NASA FIRMS)**: Ingests satellite-detected thermal anomalies (VIIRS / MODIS) with Fire Radiative Power (MW) and brightness temperatures.
8. **Natural Environmental Events (NASA EONET)**: Tracks volcanic eruptions, severe storms, floods, and sea ice extents.

## 2. Canonical Data Classes & Temporal Clocks

### Data Classes
ATLAS rigorously separates raw sensory observation from mathematical modeling:
- `OBSERVED`: A direct physical measurement recorded by a calibrated sensor or transponder (e.g. ADS-B state vector, AIS transponder report, seismic seismometer reading, VIIRS infrared thermal detection).
- `PROPAGATED`: An instantaneous position mathematically derived from earlier orbital elements using analytical or numerical propagators (e.g. SGP4 satellite propagation).
- `FORECAST`: A predictive state generated by meteorological or hydrodynamic numerical models (e.g. NOAA GFS/HRRR forecast grids).
- `REFERENCE`: Relatively static infrastructure and corporate locations (Stages 132–133).

### The Four Temporal Clocks
To prevent misleading latency metrics, ATLAS tracks four distinct timestamps for every observation:
1. `observed_at`: The physical measurement timestamp (when the transponder broadcasted or the seismometer shook). **Display age and freshness are strictly calculated from `observed_at`.**
2. `source_published_at`: The timestamp when the external provider published or made the batch available.
3. `received_at`: The timestamp when the ATLAS gateway ingested the packet.
4. `ingested_at`: The timestamp when the record was processed and committed to hot storage.

## 3. Freshness Evaluation Engine

Observations are continuously evaluated against institutional freshness policies:

| Category | Nominal Latency | Stale Threshold | Expiration Horizon |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AIRCRAFT** | 10 seconds | 120 seconds (2 min) | 900 seconds (15 min) |
| **VESSEL** | 60 seconds | 600 seconds (10 min) | 7200 seconds (2 hours) |
| **SATELLITE** | 1 second | 60 seconds | 300 seconds (5 min) |
| **WEATHER_ALERT** | 60 seconds | 14400 seconds (4 hrs) | Expiration timestamp (`expires_utc`) |
| **EARTHQUAKE** | 180 seconds | 86400 seconds (24 hrs) | 604800 seconds (7 days) |
| **ACTIVE_FIRE** | 10800 seconds (3 hrs) | 43200 seconds (12 hrs) | 86400 seconds (24 hrs) |

### Freshness States
- `CURRENT`: Age within nominal latency.
- `DELAYED`: Age exceeds nominal latency but remains below the stale threshold.
- `STALE`: Age exceeds stale threshold; downstream models must apply uncertainty discounting.
- `EXPIRED`: Observation has exceeded lifetime; purged from hot active viewport.
- `HISTORICAL`: Reconstructed state from historical temporal query.
- `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`: Upstream provider offline or unconfigured.

## 4. Invariant Protections

### Invariant 1: Watermarks & Out-of-Order Protection (Fixture C)
If an observation packet arrives with `observed_at = T1` after an observation with `observed_at = T2` (where $T1 < T2$), the hot active cache is **never rolled back**. $T1$ is archived to historical time-series storage, while the active hot state remains at $T2$.

### Invariant 2: Event Identity & Revision Deduplication (Fixture A)
When an external agency updates an ongoing event (such as USGS upgrading an earthquake from $M_w 5.2$ to $M_w 5.4$ or refining hypocenter depth), the event is **upserted in place**. The event version increments, prior snapshots are preserved in `revision_history`, and the total entity count is **never inflated**.

### Invariant 3: Trajectory Gap Splitting (Fixture D)
When flight or vessel tracking observations contain prolonged blackouts (>15 min for aircraft, >2 hours for marine vessels), ATLAS **splits the trajectory into distinct segments**. Connecting disconnected points across oceans or radar shadows with fictitious straight lines is strictly prohibited.

### Invariant 4: Non-Predictive Infrastructure Proximity (Fixture M)
Spatial correlation between live physical events (earthquakes, wildfires, hurricanes) and Stage 133 infrastructure assets (refineries, ports, power stations, data centers) strictly reports geometric distance and overlap. All correlations are assigned `damage_assessment: "UNVERIFIED_PROXIMITY_ONLY"`, `damage_confirmed: false`, and carry the mandatory disclaimer:
> *"Spatial proximity does not constitute physical damage, structural failure, or operational outage. On-site telemetry or official inspection required."*

### Invariant 5: Zero Fabrication & Fail-Closed Gating (Fixtures G, I, K)
If provider API keys (NASA FIRMS, OpenSky, Commercial AIS) are not configured, the system reports `UNCONFIGURED` or `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`. Fabricating mock flights, ships, or fires in production mode is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Orbital Mechanics (SGP4 Analytical Propagator)

The SGP4 module propagates Keplerian orbital elements to instantaneous geodetic positions:
1. **Kepler's Equation**: Solved via Newton-Raphson iteration:
   $$E - e \sin E = M(t)$$
2. **True Anomaly & Orbital Radius**:
   $$\nu = 2 \arctan\left(\sqrt{\frac{1+e}{1-e}} \tan(E/2)\right), \quad r = a(1 - e \cos E)$$
3. **$J_2$ Secular Perturbations**: Accounts for Earth oblateness causing nodal regression ($\dot{\Omega}$) and apsidal precession ($\dot{\omega}$):
   $$\dot{\Omega}_{J2} = -\frac{3}{2} J_2 \left(\frac{R_E}{p}\right)^2 n \cos(i)$$
4. **Coordinate Rotations**: Rotates perifocal coordinates to ECI, then to ECEF via Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time (GMST), and converts to WGS84 Geodetic coordinates using Bowring's method.
5. **Footprint Horizon**:
   $$\theta = \arccos\left(\frac{R_E}{R_E + h}\right), \quad s = R_E \theta$$
6. **Stale Epoch Detection (Fixture F)**: If element epoch age $> 14$ days, flags `is_stale_epoch = True`; if $> 30$ days, triggers critical ephemeris degradation warning.

## 6. REST API Endpoints

The API is mounted under `/api/v1/maps/live`:

- `GET /providers`: Returns list of all live providers with health, nominal latency, and TTL.
- `GET /providers/{id}/status`: Detailed diagnostic status for a specific provider.
- `GET /layers`: Catalog of available observation layers.
- `POST /observations/snapshot`: Active observation snapshot filtered by category and bounding box `[min_lon, min_lat, max_lon, max_lat]`.
- `POST /observations/deltas`: Cursor-based delta synchronization using sequence numbers.
- `POST /playback`: Reconstructs historical observation snapshot at `target_time_utc`.
- `POST /satellites/propagate`: Analytical SGP4 propagation from TLE elements.
- `POST /infrastructure/proximity`: Correlates live events with Stage 133 facilities.
- `POST /aggregates/h3`: Aggregates active observations into H3 hexagonal indices (resolutions 3–7).
- `POST /trajectories/split`: Splits trajectory history into segments on coverage blackouts.
- `POST /earthquakes/revision`: Demonstrates idempotent earthquake revision upserts.
