# ATLAS Modules

This document details the purpose and future feature set of the 20 core ATLAS modules.

## 1. Home
**Purpose**: Main operational command center. Not a marketing landing page. Answers: What is happening? What matters to me? What are my agents doing? Where should I go next?
**Features**:
- Global and personalized market summaries
- Portfolio overview and P&L
- Risk summary
- High-priority global events and important news
- Macroeconomic summary and upcoming economic events/earnings
- Intelligence feed
- Small God's Eye/map overview
- Active agent summary, current tasks, and pending approvals
- Alerts, recent activity, and quick launch actions

## 2. Markets
**Purpose**: Global multi-asset market discovery and monitoring.
**Features**: Equities, ETFs, funds, bonds, rates, FX, crypto, futures, commodities, indices, sectors, industries, movers, breadth, performance, heatmaps, and cross-asset views.

## 3. Charts
**Purpose**: Professional financial charting.
**Features**: Multiple chart types, indicators, drawings, volume tools, multiple layouts, synchronized charts, live and historical data, replay, saved layouts, and trading overlays.

## 4. Watchlists
**Purpose**: Persistent monitoring lists for financial entities.
**Features**: Multiple watchlists, sections, configurable columns, live updates, fundamentals, technicals, news, events, alerts, and notes.

## 5. Screeners
**Purpose**: Universal market discovery and filtering.
**Features**: Equities, ETFs, bonds, crypto, options; technical, fundamental, and quant/factor filters; saved screens, and natural-language queries.

## 6. Companies
**Purpose**: The full company intelligence profile.
**Features**: Overview, business profile, financial statements, ratios, earnings, estimates, ownership, filings, valuation, debt, competitors, suppliers, customers, supply chain, facilities, news, research, options, and geographic exposure.

## 7. Economics
**Purpose**: Global macroeconomic and central-bank intelligence.
**Features**: GDP, inflation, rates, employment, trade, debt, currencies, central banks, yield curves, country comparison, economic calendar, and forecasts.

## 8. News
**Purpose**: Real-time and historical financial/global news.
**Features**: Breaking news, market news, company news, macro, geopolitics, commodities, technology, portfolio news, entity tagging, story clustering, sentiment, importance scoring, and AI summaries.

## 9. Research
**Purpose**: Institutional-style research and document analysis.
**Features**: Filings, transcripts, presentations, academic papers, user documents, reports, semantic search, multi-document analysis, citations, notes, and AI research workflows.

## 10. Maps
**Purpose**: The geospatial and God's Eye system. Financial intelligence, not merely map visualization.
**Features**: 2D maps, 3D globe, company facilities, ports, airports, pipelines, refineries, power systems, mines, data centers, ships, flights, satellites, weather, disasters, trade flows, supply chains, portfolio exposure, and economic layers.

## 11. Intelligence
**Purpose**: Cross-domain signal and event engine.
**Features**: Anomalies, events, catalysts, narratives, macro regimes, sector rotation, unusual activity, supply-chain disruptions, geopolitical signals, intelligence scoring, event impact, portfolio relevance, and "why did this move?" explanations.

## 12. Options
**Purpose**: Dedicated derivatives analytics.
**Features**: Chains, Greeks, implied volatility, volatility surfaces, strategy builder, payoff analysis, unusual flow, and options screening.

## 13. Trading
**Purpose**: Execution and order-management experience.
**Features**: Paper trading, chart trading, order tickets, broker integrations, position management, order history, and execution analysis. Live trading will be treated as high-risk and requires permission controls.

## 14. Portfolios
**Purpose**: Portfolio management and analytics.
**Features**: Accounts, transactions, holdings, cash, valuation, P&L, performance, benchmark comparison, allocation, attribution, optimization, and geographic exposure.

## 15. Risk
**Purpose**: Portfolio and market-risk analytics.
**Features**: Volatility, beta, drawdown, Sharpe, VaR, Expected Shortfall, factor risk, concentration, FX risk, rate risk, options risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, stress testing, and geographic risk.

## 16. AI Assistant
**Purpose**: The full AI workstation for deep research and analysis.
**Features**: Long conversations, deep research, multi-step analyses, artifacts, saved conversations, data analysis, generated charts, document analysis, workflow creation, agent collaboration, tool traces, citations, and AI run history. *(Note: This is distinct from the global ATLAS AI overlay).*

## 17. Data
**Purpose**: Data-management and developer-facing data platform.
**Features**: Providers, sources, metadata, provenance, quality, freshness, APIs, SQL, exports, imports, datasets, and developer documentation.

## 18. Saved
**Purpose**: Universal personal knowledge and bookmark layer.
**Features**: Securities, companies, charts, news, research, screeners, portfolios, strategies, map scenes, workflows, AI conversations, and workspaces.

## 19. Workspaces
**Purpose**: The configurable terminal environment.
**Features**: Customizable layouts, linked widgets, resizable panels, multi-chart layouts, saved workspace templates, sharing, role-specific layouts, and future multi-monitor support.

## 20. Agents
**Purpose**: Autonomous AI employee and workflow system.
**Features**: See [AGENTS_PRODUCT_SPEC.md](AGENTS_PRODUCT_SPEC.md) for in-depth details covering Agent creation, workflows, teams, Visual Builder, and MCP integrations.
